## Description (4000)
Post a paid job in under a minute and a verified local student claims it. That's VANO.

Say what you need, out loud or in a line: "kitchen cleaned in Salthill in an hour", "walk the dog tonight", "help moving a sofa Saturday". Pin where, pick when, see the price, hold the booking fee, and you're posted. Helpers nearby see your order on a map and the first one to claim it is yours. You get a name, a photo and a rating straight away, then live updates as they head over, arrive, start and finish.

HOW IT WORKS FOR YOU
• Speak or type the job. The app fills in the fields, you check them.
• Where: use your location, drop a pin, or type the address.
• When: now, today, tonight, or pick a time.
• The price is shown before you post. VANO's small booking fee is held on your card and only charged when a helper claims. You pay your helper directly when the job is done, and they keep 100% of the job price.
• Free to cancel until a helper claims.
• Track it: Looking → Helper on the way → In progress → Done. Rate them at the end.

HOW IT WORKS FOR HELPERS
• Students in Galway sign up free, verify their ID once, and see paid orders near them on a map.
• Search "cleaning tonight" or "dog walk Salthill", filter by distance and pay, and claim with one tap. No applications, no waiting to be picked.
• Mark arrived, start and done from the job screen. Keep 100% of the job price, paid directly by the customer.

WHAT VANO IS
Same-day household help in Galway, Ireland: cleaning, garden and lawn, dog walks, laundry and ironing, moving help, furniture assembly and odd jobs. Every helper is ID-verified with a photo, a college and a rating, so you always know who's coming.

Galway-born and Irish-owned. Questions: vanojobs.com/support
